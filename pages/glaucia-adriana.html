About Glaucia Adriana

Glaucia Adriana is a Physical Therapist specialized in human movement, with a deep passion for pain neuroscience. Her journey began with Manual Therapy through the Busquet Method, and today she is a certified Structural Integrator in Anatomy Trains Structural Integration (Maine, USA) and a proud member of the International Association of Structural Integrators (IASI). She also holds certifications in Fascial Manipulation (Stecco Method), Levels 1 and 2.

In Pilates, Glaucia’s path is equally rich. She is certified by BASI Pilates (California, USA), Stott Pilates (California, USA), among other schools, that includes completing the Pilates Master Mentor Program with Lolita San Miguel (Florida, USA). What began as assisting Lolita in her programs soon turned into becoming an international educator for Lolita’s Legacy Teacher Training. Along the way, she has studied with several Elders and world-renowned teachers of the Pilates Method. Today, she travels the globe teaching, mentoring, and lecturing at international conferences, while also deepening her studies in Classical Pilates under the guidance of Brett Howard in New Jersey.

She is also the director and co-producer, alongside Voll Pilates, of the acclaimed documentary “Tribute to Joseph Pilates.” Beyond Pilates, Glaucia is a Licensed Instructor of the Gyrotonic®️ Method (Brazil Institute and New York Gyrotonic), Gyrokinesis®️, and Leg Extension Unit®️. She is also the creator of the FIM Method (Function, Integration & Movement), a unique approach that unites science, movement, and innovation in human movement.
